# Cologne - Party

### Zülpicher Straße

The student bar street in the Kwartier Latäng. Start your night here.

### [Das Ding](https://www.dingzone.de/)

Student club since 1968, Hohenstaufenring 30-32.

### [Gebäude 9](https://gebaeude9.de/)

Concerts and club nights in Deutz. The locals' pick for something less mainstream.

### Belgisches Viertel

Bars and cafés, a bit more grown-up.

## Good to know

-   Carnival season starts on 11.11. at 11:11, and the big street carnival is in February. The whole city dresses up.
-   Order a Kölsch. The waiter keeps bringing new glasses until you put your beer mat on top.
