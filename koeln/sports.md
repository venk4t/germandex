# Cologne - Sports

[UniSport Köln](https://unisport.koeln/) is the University of Cologne's sports program.

One [Sportticket](https://unisport.koeln/ueberblick/wie_kann_ich_teilnehmen/sportticket/index_ger.html) per semester opens many courses; check the current Sportticket price and eligibility. At the start of the winter semester there is a try-out period (Schnupperzeit) where you can test as many courses as you like.

Course places are limited and [booking is online only](https://unisport.koeln/ueberblick/teilnahmeregelungen/faq/index_ger.html). Each course opens on the date shown on its course page and full courses run a waitlist, so book as soon as booking opens.

## Free options

-   Run along the Rhine: flat paths on both banks.
-   Aachener Weiher and the Stadtwald for runs and summer evenings with friends.
