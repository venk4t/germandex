# Cologne - Restaurants

## Recommended

### [Habibi](https://www.habibi-koeln.de/)

Cult falafel and shawarma place on Zülpicher Straße.

### [Com Pho](https://www.comphokoeln.de/)

Vietnamese comfort food on Zülpicher Straße.

### Pastaminuta

Pizza and pasta, Luxemburger Str. 124, Sülz.

### Vegetarian? These are your best bets

Habibi is your best veg bet. Choose the falafel.

## Cheap and quick

### [Freddy Schilling](https://www.freddyschilling.de/standorte/)

Fresh burgers at three spots: Kyffhäuserstraße, Ehrenfeld and Eigelstein.

### Falafel Salam

Small falafel shop a few minutes from the university, Zülpicher Platz 17. Vegetarian.

### [Mashery Hummus Kitchen](https://www.mashery-hummus.de/)

Vegetarian hummus kitchen, Roonstraße 36. Hummus plates with pita bread; mostly vegan.
