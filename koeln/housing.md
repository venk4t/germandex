# Cologne - Housing in Cologne

Find a room. Know what you are signing.

### Start with Kölner Studierendenwerk

Apply online early. You can specify room type, move-in date and budget, but not a particular residence. Confirm the interest-check email within five days; otherwise the application is deactivated.

[Kölner Studierendenwerk housing](https://www.kstw.de/wohnen/bewerbung/)

## Keep a second search running

A residence application is not a booking. Compare shared-flat rooms and private rentals in Cologne while you wait. Check the address, the landlord or main tenant, the total monthly cost and the contract before paying.

## Before you sign

Deposits, running costs, move-in confirmation and scam checks: read the [housing guide for Germany](https://germandex.com/germany/housing) before signing.

Local source checked 1 October 2026. Check the provider for current eligibility and availability.
