# Cologne - Admin

University setup for your first week

This is not a complete arrival checklist. Address registration, health-insurance status and, where required, residence permission are separate tasks.

### 1\. Pay at the Mensa by card or phone

The old Mensakarte is gone. Pay with your bank card or your phone.

Payment failed? Try another checkout or ask staff.

### 2\. For University of Cologne students: get your digital [Deutschlandsemesterticket](https://studsek.uni-koeln.de/studium_a_bis_z/data/semesterticket/index_ger.html)

At the University of Cologne, the semester ticket is only digital (since April 2024). It covers all local and regional buses and trains in Germany. Your university explains the steps.

QR code not loading on Ride? Check enrollment and your KLIPS account, then contact the AStA (KVB's contract partner for the ticket).

-   The Deutschlandsemesterticket covers local and regional transport in second class, not ICE/IC/EC. It is funded through the semester fee, not a free ticket.
-   Do not board without a valid ticket. If your semester ticket is not ready, buy another valid fare first.

## More than university setup

### Register your Cologne address

Address registration: [city registration office (Anmeldung)](https://www.stadt-koeln.de/service/produkte/00415/index.html), within two weeks of moving in. Residence permits: [immigration authority](https://www.stadt-koeln.de/service/produkt/aufenthaltserlaubnis-zu-studienzwecken-1). Documents, health insurance, residence status and official letters: [Germany-wide admin guide](https://germandex.com/germany/admin).

You can register [fully online](https://www.stadt-koeln.de/service/produkte/00415/index.html) (no office visit needed; the address sticker arrives by post) or in person at any Kundenzentrum, no matter where in Cologne your flat is.
