# Düsseldorf - Party

### Altstadt

The "longest bar in the world": hundreds of pubs in the old town. Drink Altbier, for example at [Uerige](https://uerige.de/).

### Die Blende

Rock pub in Bilk, Friedrichstr. 122.

### [Ampere](https://ampere-club.de/programm/)

New club for concerts and parties.

### [The Eight](https://the-eight.de/)

Club on the Rhine promenade, known for big Ersti (freshers) parties in October.
