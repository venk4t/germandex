# Düsseldorf - Admin

University setup for your first week

This is not a complete arrival checklist. Address registration, health-insurance status and, where required, residence permission are separate tasks.

### 1\. For HHU students: get your [HHU Card](https://www.hhu.de/studium/bewerbung-und-einschreibung/hhu-card)

At Heinrich Heine University, the HHU Card is your student ID, Mensa card, library card and Deutschlandticket in one. It covers all local and regional buses and trains in Germany.

HHU Card not arrived? Check the postal address in the HHU portal and contact student services; two-step activation with your university ID.

### 2\. For students of other Düsseldorf universities: get a MensaCard

Students of the other universities pay at the Mensa with the Studierendenwerk's MensaCard (€2.50) and top it up in the TopUp app.

TopUp failed? Ask the Mensa checkout about other top-up options.

-   The Deutschlandsemesterticket covers local and regional transport in second class, not ICE/IC/EC. It is funded through the semester fee, not a free ticket.
-   Do not board without a valid ticket. If your semester ticket is not ready, buy another valid fare first.

## More than university setup

### Register your Düsseldorf address

Address registration: [city registration office (Anmeldung)](https://service.duesseldorf.de/suche/-/egov-bis-detail/dienstleistung/227/show), within two weeks of moving in. Residence permits: [immigration authority](https://www.duesseldorf.de/auslaenderamt). Documents, health insurance, residence status and official letters: [Germany-wide admin guide](https://germandex.com/germany/admin).

Two ways to register: fully [online](https://service.duesseldorf.de/suche/-/egov-bis-detail/dienstleistung/227/show) (ID card or EU eID card with eID activated plus its PIN; your ID is updated digitally) or in person at a Bürgerbüro - an appointment is required.
