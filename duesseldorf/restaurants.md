# Düsseldorf - Restaurants

## Recommended

### [Marukin Karaage](https://www.marukin-karaage-duesseldorf.de/)

Japanese fried chicken in the Japanese quarter around Immermannstraße.

### [Bibimcup](https://www.bibimcup.de/)

Korean bibimbap, Friedrich-Ebert-Straße 3. Tuesday to Saturday.

### [Sattgrün](https://sattgruen.de/)

Vegan buffet, at the Medienhafen (Brückenstr. 12) and in Flingern (Hoffeldstr. 18).

### Vegetarian? These are your best bets

Sattgrün is fully vegan, and Bibimcup has veg bowls.
