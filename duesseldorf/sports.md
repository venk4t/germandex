# Düsseldorf - Sports

[Hochschulsport Düsseldorf](https://hss-d.de/) runs sports for the Düsseldorf universities.

Courses at reduced rates for eligible students, booked online after you [register an account](https://hss-d.de/allgemeines-organisatorisches/teilnahme-kosten-bankverbindung/). Check eligibility and current prices before booking; popular courses can fill quickly.

## Free options

-   Run along the Rhine promenade.
-   Südpark next to the HHU campus, and the Hofgarten in the city center.
