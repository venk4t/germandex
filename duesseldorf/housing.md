# Düsseldorf - Housing in Düsseldorf

Find a room. Know what you are signing.

### Start with Studierendenwerk Düsseldorf

HHU and Hochschule Düsseldorf students are among those eligible for the Studierendenwerk residences. Choose a Düsseldorf location: the same provider also serves several other cities. It runs waiting lists and recommends applying as early as possible.

[Studierendenwerk Düsseldorf housing](https://www.stw-d.de/studentisches-wohnen/)

## Keep a second search running

A residence application is not a booking. Compare shared-flat rooms and private rentals in Düsseldorf while you wait. Check the address, the landlord or main tenant, the total monthly cost and the contract before paying.

## Before you sign

Deposits, running costs, move-in confirmation and scam checks: read the [housing guide for Germany](https://germandex.com/germany/housing) before signing.

Local source checked 1 October 2026. Check the provider for current eligibility and availability.
