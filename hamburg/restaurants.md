# Hamburg - Restaurants

## Recommended

### [Badshah](https://badshahrestaurant.de/)

Family-run Indian place since 2004, in Bremer Reihe near the main station. Known for its thalis.

### [Azeitona](https://azeitona.de/)

Falafel, hummus and mezze in the Schanze, Beckstraße 19.

### [Ibras Bistro](https://ibras-bistro-hamburg.de/)

Some of Hamburg's best falafel, Holstenstr. 185, Altona.

### Vegetarian? These are your best bets

Azeitona and Ibras are your best veg bets. Choose the falafel.

## Cheap and quick

### [Brücke 10](https://bruecke10.com/)

Fischbrötchen (fish sandwich), the Hamburg snack, right at the Landungsbrücken.
