# Hamburg - Sports

[Hochschulsport Hamburg](https://www.hochschulsport.uni-hamburg.de/) is shared by the Hamburg universities.

Courses at reduced rates for eligible students, [booked online](https://buchung.hochschulsport-hamburg.de/angebote/aktueller_zeitraum/); check eligibility and current prices before booking. The FlexiCard lets you drop in to many sessions without a fixed course.

Semester courses [open for booking online](https://www.hochschulsport.uni-hamburg.de/sportcampus/buchsys.html) on a fixed date and popular courses fill quickly, so book on the opening day.

## Free options

-   Run the loop around the Außenalster lake, about 7.5 km.
-   Stadtpark for runs and pickup games, and the Elbe beach at Övelgönne for summer evenings.
