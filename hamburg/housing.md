# Hamburg - Housing in Hamburg

Find a room. Know what you are signing.

### Start with Studierendenwerk Hamburg

You can choose up to three houses or apply for all houses. The provider says applying for all houses improves your chances. Reply to its regular interest-confirmation emails by the stated deadline to stay on the list.

[Studierendenwerk Hamburg housing](https://www.stwhh.de/wohnen/bewerbungsinformationen)

## Keep a second search running

A residence application is not a booking. Compare shared-flat rooms and private rentals in Hamburg while you wait. Check the address, the landlord or main tenant, the total monthly cost and the contract before paying.

## Before you sign

Deposits, running costs, move-in confirmation and scam checks: read the [housing guide for Germany](https://germandex.com/germany/housing) before signing.

Local source checked 1 October 2026. Check the provider for current eligibility and availability.
