# Hamburg - Party

### Reeperbahn and St. Pauli

Hamburg's famous nightlife mile. Bars, clubs and live music every night.

### Hamburger Berg

Side street off the Reeperbahn packed with cheap bars.

### Sternschanze

Bars and outdoor drinks around Schulterblatt. More relaxed than the Reeperbahn.

### [Molotow](https://molotowclub.com/programm/programm.php)

Indie club at Reeperbahn 136. Concerts and club nights.

### [Uebel & Gefährlich](https://www.uebelundgefaehrlich.com/)

Club inside an old World War II bunker at Heiligengeistfeld.

## Good to know

-   Keep an eye on your phone and wallet on the Reeperbahn on weekends.
