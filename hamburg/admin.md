# Hamburg - Admin

University setup for your first week

This is not a complete arrival checklist. Address registration, health-insurance status and, where required, residence permission are separate tasks.

### 1\. Pay at the [Mensa](https://www.stwhh.de/gastronomie/mensen-cafes-weiteres/mensa/mensa-studierendenhaus) by card or phone

The Studierendenwerk Hamburg Mensas are fully cashless. Pay with your debit or credit card, or your phone. No top-up card needed.

Card declined? Try another one or your phone; checkout staff can help.

### 2\. Get your [Deutschlandsemesterticket](https://www.hvv.de/de/service/fragen-und-antworten/semesterticket) through RIDE

Your university gives you a QR code that leads to RIDE, the campus portal for the ticket. It covers all local and regional buses and trains in Germany.

QR code not found? Check your university email and notice boards, ask the AStA or student office; the hvv FAQ explains the RIDE steps.

-   The Deutschlandsemesterticket covers local and regional transport in second class, not ICE/IC/EC. It is funded through the semester fee, not a free ticket.
-   Do not board without a valid ticket. If your semester ticket is not ready, buy another valid fare first.

## More than university setup

### Register your Hamburg address

Address registration: [city registration office (Anmeldung)](https://www.hamburg.de/service/info/111202484/), within two weeks of moving in. Residence permits: [immigration authority](https://welcome.hamburg.com/entry-and-residence/residence-permit/students-17520). Documents, health insurance, residence status and official letters: [Germany-wide admin guide](https://germandex.com/germany/admin).

You can register [fully online](https://www.hamburg.de/service/info/111202484/) or at any Kundenzentrum of your choice. Appointments are required Tuesday to Friday; on Monday and Wednesday you can walk in without one, but expect waiting times.
