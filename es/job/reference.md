# Arbeitszeugnis de ejemplo

Un Arbeitszeugnis (referencia laboral) de ejemplo, escrito en inglés. El inglés sirve para tu candidatura. Lo que lo hace funcionar en Alemania es el formato: papel con membrete de la empresa, firma, sello de la empresa y las frases de calificación fijas que buscan los reclutadores. La persona y la empresa son inventadas.

Brightpath Fintech Pvt. Ltd.

\[Company address\]  ·  \[Phone\]  ·  \[Website\]

\[City\], 31 August 20XX

### Employment Reference

Mr. Arjun Rao, born on \[date\] in \[city\], worked for our company as a Software Developer from 1 July 20XX to 31 August 20XX.

Brightpath Fintech builds digital payment products for banks and retailers, with about 150 employees.

Mr. Rao's main tasks were:

-   Developing backend services for our payment platform in Java and Spring Boot
-   Building and maintaining the mobile payment flow
-   Writing unit tests and reviewing code from other developers
-   Planning sprints together with the product and design teams

Mr. Rao has very good, well-founded technical knowledge, which he always applied with great success in his daily work. He learned new technologies very quickly and always worked independently, carefully and to a very high standard.

He always completed the tasks given to him to our fullest satisfaction.

His behavior towards managers, colleagues and customers was always exemplary. He was a valued team member at all times.

Mr. Rao is leaving our company at his own request to start a master's degree in Germany. We very much regret his departure, thank him for his always excellent work, and wish him continued success and all the best for his future, both professionally and personally.

\[Signature\]  
\[Name\], Head of Engineering  
\[Company stamp\]

Frase de calificación

"Always to our fullest satisfaction" es la nota: "fullest" + "always" = muy bien (sehr gut); "full" + "always" = bien (gut).

Frase de conducta

Comportamiento "always exemplary" con superiores, compañeros y clientes = muy bien.

Cierre

Lamentar la marcha, agradecer y desear lo mejor indica una referencia muy buena. Si falta el cierre, se lee como mala señal.
