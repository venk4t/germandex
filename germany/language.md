# Language

Even basic German _changes everything_ here: jobs, flat hunting, the Ausländerbehörde, making friends.

### Best online course: Pimsleur

For learning on your own, Pimsleur is the best online course.

### The best teacher is a part-time job

A few shifts a week in a restaurant will teach you more everyday German than any course. See Hospitality & retail jobs under [Job](https://germandex.com/job).

## Aachen

### [RWTH](https://www.sz.rwth-aachen.de/cms/sz/deutsch/kursangebot-daf/~ymonj/semesterkurse/) Sprachenzentrum

The RWTH language center. German courses from A1 to C1 for international students, during the semester and as intensive courses in the break. Register through RWTHonline.

### [VHS](https://www.vhs-aachen.de/programm/sprachen/deutsch-als-fremd-und-zweitsprache) Aachen

The city's adult education center on Peterstraße. Evening and intensive German courses at all levels.

### [Sprachenakademie](https://www.spraachen.org/en/german-courses/intensive-courses/) Aachen

Expensive, but worth it. Book the intensive course in advance so it lines up with your semester break.

### [HU](https://www.sprachenzentrum.hu-berlin.de/de/kursangebot-und-anmeldung/sprachen/deutsch) Sprachenzentrum

## Erlangen

### [FAU](https://sz.fau.de/abteilung-deutsch-als-fremdsprache/kursangebot/daf-studienbegleitende-kurse/) Sprachenzentrum

Study-accompanying German courses for enrolled students. You need a placement before you can join a course; register online in StudOn. The course list is in campo.

### [VHS](https://www.vhs-erlangen.de/programm/sprachen/allgemeine-informationen-zum-sprachenbereich) Erlangen

The city's adult education centre offers a language advice service and a free online level test. German courses are not open for trial attendance, so register in advance.

## Tübingen

### [Uni Tübingen](https://uni-tuebingen.de/international/sprachen-lernen/deutschkurse/) Deutsch als Fremdsprache

German courses for university students. Register in alma. Registration for winter semester 2026/27 had closed when we checked, but some courses still had free places.

### [VHS](https://www.vhs-tuebingen.de/kurse/deutschkurse) Tübingen

German courses from A1 to C1 every semester, with morning and evening classes, regular exams and a free placement test.
