# SCHUFA explained

### Understand SCHUFA

SCHUFA is Germany's credit record, and landlords often ask for a SCHUFA report when you apply for a flat. You do not need to open extra products to build a score: SCHUFA's own advice is to pay your bills on time and keep your finances simple. A clean payment history on the accounts you already have is what helps over time. You can check your data in the SCHUFA app ([Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.schufamobileapp) / [iPhone](https://apps.apple.com/de/app/schufa/id6448636299)) or request a free data copy from SCHUFA.

SCHUFA offers a free data copy (Datenkopie) at [schufa.de](https://www.schufa.de/datenkopie). (Source: schufa.de, checked on October 19, 2026.)
