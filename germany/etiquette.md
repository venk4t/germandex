# Etiquette

### Be quiet on public transport

Keep your voice down on buses and trains, and keep phone calls short.

### Follow traffic rules

Pedestrian crossings, traffic lights are taken very seriously!

### Be courteous

A little politeness goes a long way here. Saying "Danke" whenever you receive something is always appreciated.
