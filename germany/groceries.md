# Supermarkets in Germany

Discount and full-range supermarkets serve different needs. Compare the products you buy.

### Discount supermarkets

[Netto](https://www.netto-online.de/), [Aldi](https://www.aldi-sued.de/marken) and [Lidl](https://www.lidl.de/c/eigenmarken-food/s10007656) sell everyday groceries and own-brand products. Compare your usual basket and current offers.

### Full-range supermarkets

[Rewe](https://www.rewe-group.com/en/company/structure-and-saleslines/), [Edeka](https://verbund.edeka/unternehmen/was-ist-der-edeka-verbund/m%C3%A4rkte-vertrieb/edeka/) and [Kaufland](https://filiale.kaufland.de/sortiment.html) offer broad ranges of groceries. The selection varies by branch.

### Our editorial pick

Rewe is our pick for a full-range supermarket. This is an editorial preference, not a price ranking.
