## Parcels

### Activate Packstations on [DHL](https://www.dhl.de/de/privatkunden/pakete-empfangen/an-einem-abholort-empfangen/packstation.html) app

Delivery workers (Amazon and the rest) usually do not call you in Germany. They often leave the package outside your door or send it back. A Packstation is a locker you pick your parcels up from, and it is a reliable option for most deliveries.
