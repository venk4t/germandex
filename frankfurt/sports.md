# Frankfurt - Sports

The [Zentrum für](https://www.uni-frankfurt.de/103154514/FAQ) [Hochschulsport](https://www.uni-frankfurt.de/hochschulsport) at Goethe University is also open to Frankfurt UAS students.

Lots of courses, booked online. Courses at reduced rates for eligible students; check eligibility and current prices before booking. Popular courses can fill quickly, so check the registration dates and book early.

## Free options

-   The Mainufer: kilometers of riverside paths with a skyline view.
-   Grüneburgpark next to the Westend campus, and the Stadtwald for longer runs.
