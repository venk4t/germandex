# Frankfurt - Housing in Frankfurt

Find a room. Know what you are signing.

### Start with Studierendenwerk Frankfurt am Main

Use the Studierendenwerk online application and compare its Frankfurt residences. Read the application FAQ before sending documents or relying on a move-in date.

[Studierendenwerk Frankfurt am Main housing](https://www.swffm.de/wohnen/online-bewerbung)

## Keep a second search running

A residence application is not a booking. Compare shared-flat rooms and private rentals in Frankfurt while you wait. Check the address, the landlord or main tenant, the total monthly cost and the contract before paying.

## Before you sign

Deposits, running costs, move-in confirmation and scam checks: read the [housing guide for Germany](https://germandex.com/germany/housing) before signing.

Local source checked 1 October 2026. Check the provider for current eligibility and availability.
