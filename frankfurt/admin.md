# Frankfurt - Admin

University setup for your first week

This is not a complete arrival checklist. Address registration, health-insurance status and, where required, residence permission are separate tasks.

### 1\. Pay at the Mensa with your university card

The Studierendenwerk Frankfurt Mensas take your university card or a MensaCard.

No balance on the card? Ask the Mensa checkout about top-ups or a MensaCard.

### 2\. For Goethe University students: get your Deutschlandsemesterticket

At Goethe University it is a digital ticket from the [AStA's online portal](https://asta-frankfurt.de/angebote/kultur-mobilitaet/semesterticket). It covers all local and regional buses and trains in Germany.

No digital ticket? Check your re-registration (Rückmeldung) and semester fee - the ticket is paid with that fee - then contact the AStA ticket support.

-   The Deutschlandsemesterticket covers local and regional transport in second class, not ICE/IC/EC. It is funded through the semester fee, not a free ticket.
-   Do not board without a valid ticket. If your semester ticket is not ready, buy another valid fare first.

### 3\. For Frankfurt UAS students: activate your Deutschlandsemesterticket

At Frankfurt University of Applied Sciences the ticket is paid with the semester fee and is digital only. Activate it in the [AStA ticket portal](https://asta-fra-uas.de/semesterticketbuero/) with your university account, keep it in your phone wallet and carry photo ID. It is renewed each semester.

No smartphone? Ask the AStA semester ticket office about the chip-card process.

## More than university setup

### Register your Frankfurt address

Address registration: [city registration office (Anmeldung)](https://frankfurt.de/service-und-rathaus/verwaltung/aemter-und-institutionen/buergeramt-statistik-und-wahlen/buergeraemter/terminservice), within two weeks of moving in. Residence permits: [immigration authority](https://frankfurt.de/auslaenderangelegenheiten). Documents, health insurance, residence status and official letters: [Germany-wide admin guide](https://germandex.com/germany/admin).

You can register a main residence [fully online](https://frankfurt.de/service-und-rathaus/verwaltung/aemter-und-institutionen/buergeramt-statistik-und-wahlen/buergeraemter/terminservice) (via wohnsitzanmeldung.gov.de) or in person at any Bürgeramt location, no matter where in Frankfurt you live - an appointment is required.
