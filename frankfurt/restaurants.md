# Frankfurt - Restaurants

## Recommended

### [Bun Bo](https://bun-bo-restaurant-frankfurt.eatbu.com/?lang=de)

Vietnamese noodles and bowls, in the Ostend.

### [Pizzeria Montana](https://www.montana-pizzeria.de/allergene-zusatzstoffe/)

Neapolitan pizza, Weserstraße 14.

### [Apfelwein Wagner](https://www.apfelwein-wagner.com/)

Classic apple wine tavern since 1931, Schweizer Str. 71, Sachsenhausen. Try the Grüne Soße.

### Vegetarian? These are your best bets

Order the Grüne Soße with potatoes and eggs at Apfelwein Wagner, or a margherita at Montana.
