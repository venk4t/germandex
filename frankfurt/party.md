# Frankfurt - Party

### Alt-Sachsenhausen

Old pub quarter with apple wine taverns like Zur Buchscheer.

### [Gibson](https://gibson-club.de/)

Club with a live stage on the Zeil.

### [Batschkapp](https://www.batschkapp.net/)

Concerts and parties, Gwinnerstraße 5.

### [Tanzhaus West](https://tanzhaus-west.de/)

Techno and house in an old factory.

## Good to know

-   For student bars, head to Bockenheim (the old university quarter), Nordend and Bornheim.
