# Berlin - Admin

University setup for your first week

This is not a complete arrival checklist. Address registration, health-insurance status and, where required, residence permission are separate tasks.

### 1\. Load your MensaCard before your first lunch

Berlin's Mensas (run by the Studierendenwerk Berlin) only take the MensaCard or your university's CampusCard. No cash, no bank cards.

No MensaCard yet? Get one at the [Mensa](https://www.stw.berlin/mensen/faq-mensen/mensacard.html) checkout and top up at the machines; bring your student card or CampusCard.

### 2\. For [FU](https://www.fu-berlin.de/studium/studieren/studienorganisation/semesterticket/Deutschlandsemesterticket/Wie-erhalte-ich-Ticket.html) Berlin students: activate your Deutschlandsemesterticket

At FU Berlin the ticket is only available in the S-Bahn Berlin app, and the steps must be repeated every semester. Other universities send their own steps to your university email.

No ticket in the S-Bahn app? Check enrollment and re-registration, then contact the AStA FU. Steps repeat every semester.

-   The Deutschlandsemesterticket covers local and regional transport in second class, not ICE/IC/EC. It is funded through the semester fee, not a free ticket.
-   Do not board without a valid ticket. If your semester ticket is not ready, buy another valid fare first.

## More than university setup

### Register your Berlin address

Address registration: [city registration office (Anmeldung)](https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/120686/), within two weeks of moving in. Residence permits: [immigration authority](https://www.berlin.de/einwanderung/en/residence/study/). Documents, health insurance, residence status and official letters: [Germany-wide admin guide](https://germandex.com/germany/admin).

Two ways to register: fully [online via BundID](https://service.berlin.de/dienstleistung/120686/) if you have a German ID card or EU eID card with the eID function activated and its PIN, or in person at a Bürgeramt with an appointment. Book with the [city-wide search](https://service.berlin.de/terminvereinbarung/hinweise/) - it covers every Bürgeramt, not only your district. Offices typically load new slots early in the morning, a new booking day opens daily at opening time, and cancelled slots reappear - check back regularly.
