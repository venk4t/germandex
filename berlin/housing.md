# Berlin - Housing in Berlin

Find a room. Know what you are signing.

### Start with studierendenWERK BERLIN

Use the Housing Portal to apply and manage your application. Check the list of participating Berlin universities and the current contact notice before applying.

[studierendenWERK BERLIN housing](https://www.stw.berlin/en/housing/housing-portal.html)

## Keep a second search running

A residence application is not a booking. Compare shared-flat rooms and private rentals in Berlin while you wait. Check the address, the landlord or main tenant, the total monthly cost and the contract before paying.

## Before you sign

Deposits, running costs, move-in confirmation and scam checks: read the [housing guide for Germany](https://germandex.com/germany/housing) before signing.

Local source checked 1 October 2026. Check the provider for current eligibility and availability.
