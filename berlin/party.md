# Berlin - Party

### [Simon-Dach-Straße](https://www.visitberlin.de/en/going-out-simon-dach-strasse)

One of Berlin's best-known bar streets, in Friedrichshain. Good place to start the night.

### RAW-Gelände

Old railway yard on Revaler Straße with clubs like Cassiopeia (hip hop, concerts).

### [Matrix](https://www.matrix-berlin.de/)

Big mainstream club at Warschauer Brücke. Party every night, €5 for students before midnight.

### Weserstraße

Neukölln's bar street. Cheap drinks, lots of choice.

## Good to know

-   Berlin clubs have strict doors and usually ban photos inside. Go in small groups and don't take a no personally.
-   [Student parties](https://www.studentenparty-berlin.de/) are listed on studentenparty-berlin.de.
