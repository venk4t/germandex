# Berlin - Sports

Every big university runs its own Hochschulsport: [FU](https://www.fu-sport.de/), [HU](https://www.hochschulsport.hu-berlin.de/de/angebot/sportangebot) and [TU](https://www.tu-sport.de/).

Hundreds of courses, booked online on your university's sports site. Courses at reduced rates for eligible students; check eligibility and current prices before booking. Popular courses can fill quickly, so check the registration dates and book early.

## Free options

-   Tempelhofer Feld: the old airport, now a huge open field for running, cycling and skating.
-   Tiergarten and Volkspark Friedrichshain for runs and pickup football.
