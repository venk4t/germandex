# Berlin - Restaurants

## Recommended

### [Mustafa's](https://mustafas.de/) Gemüse Kebap

Berlin's most famous veggie kebab, at Mehringdamm, Kreuzberg. Expect a queue.

### [Monsieur Vuong](https://www.monsieurvuong.de/)

Family-run Vietnamese kitchen, Alte Schönhauser Str. 46, Mitte.

### [Konnopke's](https://www.konnopke-imbiss.de/) Imbiss

Currywurst since 1930, under the U-Bahn tracks at Schönhauser Allee.

### Vegetarian? These are your best bets

Mustafa's is the veg classic. Most Vietnamese places have tofu versions of their dishes.

## Cheap and quick

### [Curry 36](https://curry36.de/mehringdamm/)

Currywurst at Mehringdamm 36, Kreuzberg. Open daily until 5 am.

### Burgermeister

Burgers from an old public toilet under the U-Bahn at Schlesisches Tor (Oberbaumstraße 8).
