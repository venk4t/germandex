# Berlin - Supermarkets

For the chains and how to choose, read our [supermarket guide for Germany](https://germandex.com/germany/groceries). Local international stores are below.

## Asian & international supermarkets

### [Zora](https://zorastore.eu/de/pages/zora-kreuzberg) Supermarkt

Kottbusser Damm 93, Kreuzberg. Indian, Asian, African and Latin American groceries. Also a branch in Mitte.

### [Vinh-Loi](https://www.vinhloi.de/stores)

Asian supermarket, Ansbacher Str. 16, Schöneberg. More branches around the city.

### Dong Xuan Center

Herzbergstraße, Lichtenberg. Berlin's largest Asian market: six halls of groceries, restaurants and more.
