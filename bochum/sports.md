# Bochum - Sports

[Hochschulsport Bochum](https://buchung.hochschulsport.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/angebote/aktueller_zeitraum/) is run by [Ruhr University](https://hochschulsport-bochum.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/de/faqs-oft-gestellte-fragen).

You register online for each course before you join. Courses are usually cheaper for eligible students; check eligibility and current prices before booking.

## Free options

-   Kemnader See, the lake near the campus. There is a free 5 km parkrun every Saturday morning.
