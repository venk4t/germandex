# Bochum - Housing in Bochum

Find a room. Know what you are signing.

### Start with AKAFÖ

AKAFÖ offers shared rooms, apartments and family housing around its university locations. Compare furnished and unfurnished options and check whether your university is in its service area before applying.

[AKAFÖ housing](https://www.akafoe.de/wohnen/mietinteressenten)

## Keep a second search running

A residence application is not a booking. Compare shared-flat rooms and private rentals in Bochum while you wait. Check the address, the landlord or main tenant, the total monthly cost and the contract before paying.

## Before you sign

Deposits, running costs, move-in confirmation and scam checks: read the [housing guide for Germany](https://germandex.com/germany/housing) before signing.

Local source checked 1 October 2026. Check the provider for current eligibility and availability.
