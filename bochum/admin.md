# Bochum - Admin

University setup for your first week

This is not a complete arrival checklist. Address registration, health-insurance status and, where required, residence permission are separate tasks.

### 1\. For [RUB](https://www.it-services.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/services/sl/semesterticket.html.de) students: pay at the Mensa with your chip card

Akafö Mensas take your student ID (it has a chip) or a MensaCard. Load money onto it before your first lunch.

Chip failed at checkout? Ask Akafö staff about a backup MensaCard.

### 2\. For RUB students: activate your Deutschland-Semesterticket

Ruhr University explains the steps on its IT services page. It covers all local and regional buses and trains in Germany, so Dortmund and Essen are a short ride away.

Ticket missing? Check enrollment and semester fee payment, then follow RUB IT services or contact the AStA.

-   The Deutschlandsemesterticket covers local and regional transport in second class, not ICE/IC/EC. It is funded through the semester fee, not a free ticket.
-   Do not board without a valid ticket. If your semester ticket is not ready, buy another valid fare first.

## More than university setup

### Register your Bochum address

Address registration: [city registration office (Anmeldung)](https://www.bochum.de/Buergerbuero/Dienstleistungen-und-Infos/Anmeldung-in-Bochum), within two weeks of moving in. Residence permits: [immigration authority](https://www.bochum.de/Auslaenderbuero). Documents, health insurance, residence status and official letters: [Germany-wide admin guide](https://germandex.com/germany/admin).

You can register a main residence [fully online](https://www.bochum.de/Buergerbuero/Dienstleistungen-und-Infos/Anmeldung-in-Bochum) (via wohnsitzanmeldung.gov.de) or in person at a Bürgerbüro with an appointment. If slots run past your two-week deadline, booking the appointment already counts as registering on time - bring the confirmation.
