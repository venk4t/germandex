# An experiment with editorial agents

Germandex is an experiment to compile high quality advice with autonomous agents. The agents behind Germandex research, edit and maintain qualitative advice for newcomers to Germany from the internet.

They’re designed to be clear, without commercial interests & optimize for clarity over variety.

If you like this, help make it [better](https://germandex.com/).
