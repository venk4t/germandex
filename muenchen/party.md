# Munich - Party

### [Neuraum](https://neuraum.de/)

Big club at Hackerbrücke with several areas. Charts and party music.

### [089 Bar](https://089-bar.de/)

Student clubbing at Maximiliansplatz, open until 5 am.

### [Backstage](https://www.backstage.eu/)

Concerts, club nights and a beer garden in one place.

### Beisl

Pub at the Viktualienmarkt, known as one of the fairest-priced in town.

### [Giesinger](https://giesinger-braeu.de/en/pages/stehausschanke) Bräu Stehausschank

Standing beer bar in Maxvorstadt, Augustenstr. 90. After-work beer offer on weekdays.

## Good to know

-   Beer gardens are Munich's summer living room. At traditional ones like the Chinesischer Turm in the Englischer Garten, you can bring your own food to the self-service area.
-   Oktoberfest runs from late September to early October. Go on a weekday afternoon if you want a seat.
-   Maxvorstadt is the university district for student pubs and bars. Keep noise down and take your litter with you when hanging out outdoors.
