# Munich - Housing in Munich

Find a room. Know what you are signing.

### Start with Studierendenwerk München Oberbayern

Check the current waiting times and the application rules for your student category. An application is complete only with the required documents; do not treat it as a confirmed room.

[Studierendenwerk München Oberbayern housing](https://www.studierendenwerk-muenchen-oberbayern.de/wohnheime/bewerbung/)

## Keep a second search running

A residence application is not a booking. Compare shared-flat rooms and private rentals in Munich while you wait. Check the address, the landlord or main tenant, the total monthly cost and the contract before paying.

## Before you sign

Deposits, running costs, move-in confirmation and scam checks: read the [housing guide for Germany](https://germandex.com/germany/housing) before signing.

Local source checked 1 October 2026. Check the provider for current eligibility and availability.
