# Munich - Sports

The [ZHS](https://kurse.zhs-muenchen.de/de) ([Zentraler Hochschulsport München](https://www.zhs-muenchen.de/zhs/faqs/alle-infos-rund-um-die-sportkurse/)) runs sports for all Munich universities.

A huge program, booked online on the ZHS site. Courses at reduced rates for eligible students; check eligibility and current prices before booking. Popular courses can fill quickly, so check the registration dates and book early.

## Free options

-   Englischer Garten: one of the biggest city parks in the world, great for runs.
-   The Isar riverbanks and the Olympiapark for jogging, cycling and summer evenings.
