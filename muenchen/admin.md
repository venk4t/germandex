# Munich - Admin

University setup for your first week

This is not a complete arrival checklist. Address registration, health-insurance status and, where required, residence permission are separate tasks.

### 1\. For LMU and TUM students: turn on Autoload for your StudentCard

LMU and TUM students pay at the [Mensa](https://www.studierendenwerk-muenchen-oberbayern.de/en/gastronomie/preise-und-bezahlung/) with the StudentCard. Turn on Autoload so it tops itself up. Some Mensas also take bank cards.

No Autoload? Top up your StudentCard at the Mensa machines.

### 2\. There is no semester ticket in Munich

The student contract was suspended in 2023, and your student card is not a transport ticket. Get the Bayerisches Ermäßigungsticket instead: a [Deutschlandticket](https://www.mvg.de/abos-tickets/abos/ermaessigungsticket.html) for students for €43 a month.

Not eligible for the Bayerisches Ermäßigungsticket? Compare the regular Deutschlandticket; MVG lists current price and conditions.

-   Do not board without a valid ticket. If your semester ticket is not ready, buy another valid fare first.

## More than university setup

### Register your Munich address

Address registration: [city registration office (Anmeldung)](https://stadt.muenchen.de/service/info/wohnsitzanmeldung/1063475/n0/), within two weeks of moving in. Residence permits: [immigration authority](https://stadt.muenchen.de/service/info/studenten/1089339/). Documents, health insurance, residence status and official letters: [Germany-wide admin guide](https://germandex.com/germany/admin).

Registering a main residence can also be done fully [online](https://stadt.muenchen.de/service/info/wohnsitzanmeldung/1063475/n0/) if you have an ID with the activated online-ID (eID) function, its six-digit PIN and an NFC-capable smartphone with the AusweisApp. Otherwise, register in person: any Bürgerbüro in the city works, not only your district office. Appointments are required for the in-person visit, but [new slots are released several times a day](https://stadt.muenchen.de/infos/terminvereinbarung-buergerbueros.html), including same-day slots about 30 minutes before offices open - check repeatedly.
