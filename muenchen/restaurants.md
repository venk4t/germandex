# Munich - Restaurants

## Recommended

### [Sababa](https://www.sababa-munich.com/)

Falafel, hummus and shawarma right at the Viktualienmarkt, Westenriederstr. 9.

### [Lo Studente](https://lo-studente.de/)

Wood-fired pizza in the university quarter, Schellingstr. 30. Open daily until midnight.

### [Bánh Mì Minh](https://banhmi-minh.de/)

Vietnamese sandwiches, Theresienstr. 79.

### Vegetarian? These are your best bets

Sababa and MOGO are your best veg bets. Pick the falafel or the hummus.

## Cheap and quick

### [MOGO](https://www.mogo-munchen.de/)

Lebanese street food, hummus and wraps, Augustenstr. 93.

### Taco Company

Tacos and burritos near LMU, Amalienstr. 69.

### [Asia Bistro Minh](http://asia-bistrominh.de/)

Vietnamese, Thai and Chinese dishes, Amalienstr. 37. Takeaway near LMU.

## Indian restaurants

### [Dhaba Münchner Freiheit](https://www.dhaba.de/dhabamunchnerfreiheit)

Indian curries and tandoori in Schwabing, Herzogstr. 1b. Sit-down restaurant near Münchner Freiheit.
