# Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

## Short version

No advertising cookies, tracking pixels or ads. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookieless tool that does not track individual visitors. Cloudflare Pages delivers these pages, and Cloudflare Turnstile checks the feedback form for bots.

## What germandex collects

Only what you choose to send through the feedback form, plus anonymous page-view statistics. There are no visitor accounts, advertising cookies, tracking pixels or ads, and germandex does not share or sell any personal data. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views and visits without cookies: it records the page viewed, the country, device and browser type, and the referring site, and it does not identify or follow individual visitors across websites. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in knowing which guides are read (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## What stays in your browser

Your city choice is saved in your browser's local storage (under the name "gx-city") so the site remembers it next time. This data stays on your device and is never sent to germandex or anyone else. Your language is part of the page address (for example germandex.com/fr/), not stored. You can delete the stored city at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages (Cloudflare, Inc.). When you open a page, Cloudflare's servers process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. This is needed to deliver the pages and keep them secure, and Cloudflare may process it outside the EU, including in the US. The legal basis is the legitimate interest in running the site reliably and safely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) for details.

## Fonts, images and bot protection

Fonts and guide images are served from this site. When you open the feedback form, Cloudflare Turnstile checks whether the visitor is human. It processes technical signals such as the IP address, browser information and TLS fingerprint for security, not advertising. See [Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/). We use a daily salted digest of the connection IP address to limit repeated form attempts, not the raw IP in feedback records. Links to other websites take you to sites with their own privacy policies.

## Feedback form

The "Suggest Edits" button and the "better" link in the manifesto open a form. germandex receives your message, any screenshot you attach, your optional reply email, the page you were on and your language setting. Cloudflare Workers processes the form; Cloudflare D1 stores the feedback record, and a private Cloudflare R2 bucket in the European Union stores screenshots. Screenshots are not publicly listed. The notification email includes your message and a screenshot link that expires after 24 hours. Email notifications are sent from feedback@germandex.com to germandexagent@gmail.com, a mailbox Venkat runs for germandex, through Cloudflare's verified-destination email service. Stored feedback is checked for delivery hourly. If email delivery is temporarily unavailable, the feedback remains queued in Cloudflare rather than being discarded.

Your email is used only to reply to your message, not added to a mailing list. The basis for processing the feedback is your consent when you send the form (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Bot checks and rate limits protect the service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Screenshots are set to expire from R2 automatically after 30 days. Screenshot links stop working sooner, after 24 hours. The message and optional reply email in D1 are kept until the feedback has been dealt with and deleted; they do not currently have automatic 30-day deletion. Email copies, once notifications are enabled, remain in Venkat's inbox until deleted. Cloudflare D1's Free plan also keeps recovery history for up to seven days after active records are deleted. You can request deletion using the contact below.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to complain to a data protection supervisory authority. Unless you have sent the feedback form, germandex holds no personal data about you. To access or delete a submission, get in touch using the contact below. For data processed by the host, see Cloudflare's privacy policy.
