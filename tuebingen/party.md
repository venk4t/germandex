# Tübingen - Party

### [Kuckuck](https://kuckuck-bar.de/)

Bar and club run by students for students. Its programme lists karaoke, pub quiz, beer pong and themed nights.

### [Sudhaus](https://www.sudhaus-tuebingen.de/veranstaltungen/party.html)

Venue at Hechinger Straße 203 with regular party nights, for example Zeitgeist with music from the 70s, 80s and 90s.

## Good to know

-   Programmes and entry prices change every week. Check the venue's own page on the day you go.

Local sources checked 22 October 2026. Check the linked pages for current prices and rules.
