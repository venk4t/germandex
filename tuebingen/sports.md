# Tübingen - Sports

## For University of Tübingen students: Hochschulsport

[Hochschulsport Tübingen](https://uni-tuebingen.de/einrichtungen/zentrale-einrichtungen/hochschulsport/home/) offers more than 350 courses each semester. Book online on the [booking page](https://buchung.hsp.uni-tuebingen.de/angebote/aktueller_zeitraum/m.html).

The page announces registration for winter semester 2026/27, with tennis courses opening for registration on 23 October 2026 at 17:00.

The Hochschulsport office is at Wilhelmstr. 124.

### Check the current [registration dates](https://buchung.hsp.uni-tuebingen.de/angebote/aktueller_zeitraum/m.html)

Popular courses may fill quickly. Registration times differ by course; check the booking page before setting an alarm.

Local sources checked 22 October 2026. Check the linked pages for current prices and rules.
