# Tübingen - Restaurants

## On campus

### [Mensa Wilhelmstraße](https://www.my-stuwe.de/mensa/mensa-wilhelmstrasse-tuebingen/)

Studierendenwerk dining hall at Wilhelmstraße 13. The daily menu is on its page. The Studierendenwerk says allergen and additive labels are shown only at the serving counters, so check there.

### [Mensa Morgenstelle](https://www.my-stuwe.de/mensa/mensa-morgenstelle-tuebingen/)

Another Studierendenwerk Mensa in Tübingen. Check its page for the current menu and hours.

### [Asien-Haus Imbiss](https://www.asien-haus.net/)

Thai takeaway counter in the old town, Schmiedtorstraße 7 and 11: noodle soup and daily changing dishes at lunchtime, Monday to Friday 11:30 to 15:00, according to its site.

### Vegetarian or vegan?

Mensa menus mark vegetarian and vegan dishes. Check the menu for the day.

Off campus: we have not verified any restaurant for this city against a primary source yet, so we list none rather than guess. Checked 22 October 2026.
