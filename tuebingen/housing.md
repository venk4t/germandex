# Tübingen - Housing in Tübingen

Find a room. Know what you are signing.

### Start with Studierendenwerk Tübingen-Hohenheim

You can only apply online. You can apply before you have your admission letter, but send it as soon as you get it. Winter-semester applications open on 1 April and summer-semester applications on 1 October of the year before. Couples and families need one application per adult, and at least one partner must be admitted or enrolled.

[Studierendenwerk Tübingen-Hohenheim housing](https://www.my-stuwe.de/wohnen/online-bewerbung/)

## Keep a second search running

A residence application is not a booking. Compare shared-flat rooms and private rentals while you wait. Check the address, the landlord or main tenant, the total monthly cost and the contract before paying.

## Before you sign

Deposits, running costs, move-in confirmation and scam checks: read the [housing guide for Germany](https://germandex.com/germany/housing) before signing.

Local sources checked 22 October 2026. Check the linked pages for current prices and rules.
