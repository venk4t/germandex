# Tübingen - Medical help in Tübingen

The right help, without guessing where to go.

### Find a doctor near you

Use the KV Baden-Württemberg practice search with Tübingen or your postcode. Start with a Hausarzt for general care; check the practice's contact details and ask about appointments and your insurance before going.

[Find a doctor in Tübingen](https://www.kvbawue.de/patienten/praxissuche)

## Urgent or out of hours?

During office hours, contact a Hausarzt (general practitioner). A local Apotheke (pharmacy) can advise on medicines. For urgent illness that cannot wait until the practice reopens but is not life-threatening, call 116117. For life-threatening emergencies or possible lasting harm, call 112. When unsure whether life is at risk, call 112.

[Medical help and emergency numbers](https://gesund.bund.de/notfallnummern)

## Hospital emergency department

The Universitätsklinikum Tübingen lists its Zentrale Notaufnahme at Hoppe-Seyler-Str. 3. Outside practice hours you can also call 116117, use docdirekt or visit an emergency practice at the Klinikum. Poisoning: Giftzentrale Baden-Württemberg, 0761 192-40. For life-threatening emergencies call 112.

[Universitätsklinikum Tübingen emergency page](https://www.medizin.uni-tuebingen.de/de/kontakt/notfall)

## Pharmacies in Tübingen

Use the emergency-pharmacy search with your Tübingen address or postcode. The duty rota changes; check the current result before travelling.

[Find an emergency pharmacy](https://www.aponet.de/notdienstsuche/-)
