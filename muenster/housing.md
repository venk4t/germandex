# Münster - Housing in Münster

Find a room. Know what you are signing.

### Start with Studierendenwerk Münster

New students can apply without an admission letter. Choose up to three residences carefully: the choice cannot later be changed. Watch for email offers and reply; ignoring an offer can delete your application.

[Studierendenwerk Münster housing](https://www.stw-muenster.de/wohnen/bewerbung-wohnraum/)

## Keep a second search running

A residence application is not a booking. Compare shared-flat rooms and private rentals in Münster while you wait. Check the address, the landlord or main tenant, the total monthly cost and the contract before paying.

## Before you sign

Deposits, running costs, move-in confirmation and scam checks: read the [housing guide for Germany](https://germandex.com/germany/housing) before signing.

Local source checked 1 October 2026. Check the provider for current eligibility and availability.
