# Münster - Medical help in Münster

The right help, without guessing where to go.

### Find a doctor near you

Use KV Westfalen-Lippe's practice search with Münster or your postcode. Start with a Hausarzt for general medical care; check the practice's contact details and ask about appointments and your insurance before going.

[Find a doctor in Münster](https://www.kvwl.de/arztsuche)

## Urgent or out of hours?

During office hours, contact a Hausarzt (general practitioner). A local Apotheke (pharmacy) can advise on medicines. For urgent illness that cannot wait until the practice reopens but is not life-threatening, call 116117. For life-threatening emergencies or possible lasting harm, call 112. When unsure whether life is at risk, call 112.

[Medical help and emergency numbers](https://gesund.bund.de/notfallnummern)

## Pharmacies in Münster

Use the emergency-pharmacy search with your Münster address or postcode. The duty rota changes; check the current result before travelling.

[Find an emergency pharmacy](https://www.aponet.de/notdienstsuche/-)
