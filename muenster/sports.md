# Münster - Sports

[Hochschulsport Münster](https://www.uni-muenster.de/Hochschulsport/) offers a wide range of sports.

Flexible memberships ([Sportpass](https://www.uni-muenster.de/Hochschulsport/mitglied/mitgliedschaften.shtml)) instead of single courses, plus the Campus Gym. Book online, and check current membership prices and eligibility first.

## Free options

-   Run around the Aasee lake.
-   The Promenade: a green ring around the old town, for bikes and walkers only.
