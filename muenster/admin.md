# Münster - Admin

University setup for your first week

This is not a complete arrival checklist. Address registration, health-insurance status and, where required, residence permission are separate tasks.

### 1\. Get a bike

The bike ("Leeze" in Münster) is the main way to get around. The city is built for it.

### 2\. Pay at the Mensa without cash

The Mensas are cashless. Pay with your student card, a MensaCard or your bank card.

Payment failed? Try another checkout or ask staff.

### 3\. For University of [Münster](https://www.uni-muenster.de/studium/orga/studienverwaltung/selfservice.html) students: download your semester ticket

At the University of Münster you get it in the SelfService portal. It covers all local and regional buses and trains in Germany.

No ticket in SelfService? Check re-registration and semester fee, then contact student administration.

-   The Deutschlandsemesterticket covers local and regional transport in second class, not ICE/IC/EC. It is funded through the semester fee, not a free ticket.
-   Do not board without a valid ticket. If your semester ticket is not ready, buy another valid fare first.

## More than university setup

### Register your Münster address

Address registration: [city registration office (Anmeldung)](https://www.stadt-muenster.de/buergerservice/meldeangelegenheiten/anmeldung), within two weeks of moving in. Residence permits: [immigration authority](https://www.stadt-muenster.de/aufenthaltsrecht/studium-und-promotion/waehrend-des-studiums). Documents, health insurance, residence status and official letters: [Germany-wide admin guide](https://germandex.com/germany/admin).

You can register [fully online](https://www.stadt-muenster.de/buergerservice/meldeangelegenheiten/anmeldung) (your ID chip is updated and the address sticker arrives by post) or in person with an appointment at a Bürgerbüro or Bezirksverwaltung.
