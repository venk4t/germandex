# Münster - Restaurants

## Recommended

### [Cavete](https://cavete-muenster.de/)

Münster's "academic beer house" in the Kuhviertel, Kreuzstraße 37/38. Pasta and beer.

### [Pinkus Müller](https://www.pinkus.de/)

Brewery pub since 1816, Kreuzstr. 7-10. Westphalian food.

### [Das Blaue Haus](https://www.blaue-haus-muenster.de/)

Student pub with Westphalian food, Kreuzstraße 16-17.
