# Dortmund - Admin

University setup for your first week

This is not a complete arrival checklist. Address registration, health-insurance status and, where required, residence permission are separate tasks.

### 1\. Pay at the Mensa by card or phone

Pay contactless with your bank card or your phone.

Contactless payment failed? Try another checkout or ask Studierendenwerk staff.

### 2\. For [TU Dortmund](https://www.tu-dortmund.de/semesterticket/) students: get your Deutschlandsemesterticket

TU Dortmund gives it to you together with the AStA. It covers all local and regional buses and trains in Germany.

Ticket not issued? Check enrollment and semester fee, then contact the AStA or student services.

-   The Deutschlandsemesterticket covers local and regional transport in second class, not ICE/IC/EC. It is funded through the semester fee, not a free ticket.
-   Do not board without a valid ticket. If your semester ticket is not ready, buy another valid fare first.

## More than university setup

### Register your Dortmund address

Address registration: [city registration office (Anmeldung)](https://www.dortmund.de/services/wohnsitzanmeldung.html), within two weeks of moving in. Residence permits: [immigration authority](https://application.dortmund.de/rathaus-und-verwaltung/verwaltung/amt-fuer-migration/kontakt/). Documents, health insurance, residence status and official letters: [Germany-wide admin guide](https://germandex.com/germany/admin).

You can register [fully online](https://www.dortmund.de/services/wohnsitzanmeldung.html) (eID function of your ID card plus a free BundID account) or in person with an appointment.
