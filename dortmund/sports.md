# Dortmund - Sports

[Hochschulsport Dortmund](https://hsp.tu-dortmund.de/sportangebot/) is run by TU Dortmund.

First buy a [Sportkarte](https://www.buchsys.ahs.tu-dortmund.de/angebote/aktueller_zeitraum/_SPORTKARTE.html), then book your courses online. Check the current Sportkarte price and your eligibility before buying.

## Free options

-   Phoenix-See: flat, wide paths around the lake.
