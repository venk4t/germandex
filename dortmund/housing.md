# Dortmund - Housing in Dortmund

Find a room. Know what you are signing.

### Start with Studierendenwerk Dortmund

Start with the Studierendenwerk housing pages for its residences, current offers and application FAQ. Check the application conditions for your university and keep an independent room search running alongside it.

[Studierendenwerk Dortmund housing](https://www.stwdo.de/wohnen)

## Keep a second search running

A residence application is not a booking. Compare shared-flat rooms and private rentals in Dortmund while you wait. Check the address, the landlord or main tenant, the total monthly cost and the contract before paying.

## Before you sign

Deposits, running costs, move-in confirmation and scam checks: read the [housing guide for Germany](https://germandex.com/germany/housing) before signing.

Local source checked 1 October 2026. Check the provider for current eligibility and availability.
