# Dortmund - Restaurants

## Recommended

### Mo's Beef

Burgers, Lortzingstraße 28.

### [Al Afandi](https://www.al-afandi-dortmund.de/)

Arabic food in the city center, Ostenhellweg 40.

### [Little Italy](https://www.little-italy-dortmund.de/)

Italian at Hansastraße 44. Most dishes cost no more than €10.

## Cheap and quick

### [Wurst Willi](https://www.wurstwilli.com/)

Currywurst at Petrikirchhof. Monday to Saturday.
