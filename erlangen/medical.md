# Erlangen - Medical help in Erlangen

The right help, without guessing where to go.

### Find a doctor near you

Use the 116117 doctor search with Erlangen or your postcode. Start with a Hausarzt for general care; check the practice's contact details and ask about appointments and your insurance before going.

[Find a doctor in Erlangen](https://arztsuche.116117.de/)

## Urgent or out of hours?

During office hours, contact a Hausarzt (general practitioner). A local Apotheke (pharmacy) can advise on medicines. For urgent illness that cannot wait until the practice reopens but is not life-threatening, call 116117. For life-threatening emergencies or possible lasting harm, call 112. When unsure whether life is at risk, call 112.

[Medical help and emergency numbers](https://gesund.bund.de/notfallnummern)

## Hospital emergency departments

Uniklinikum Erlangen lists emergency departments round the clock: surgery at Krankenhausstr. 12 (entrance at Maximiliansplatz), tel. 09131 85-33260; internal medicine at Ulmenweg 18, tel. 09131 85-35420; children at Loschgestr. 15, tel. 09131 85-33118. For life-threatening emergencies call 112.

[Uniklinikum Erlangen emergency departments](https://www.uk-erlangen.de/944/)

## Pharmacies in Erlangen

Use the emergency-pharmacy search with your Erlangen address or postcode. The duty rota changes; check the current result before travelling.

[Find an emergency pharmacy](https://www.aponet.de/notdienstsuche/-)
