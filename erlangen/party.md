# Erlangen - Party

### [Zirkel](https://zirkel-club.de/)

Student club and disco at Hauptstraße 105 in the old town. The next nights are listed on its site.

### [E-Werk Kulturzentrum](https://www.e-werk.de/das-kulturzentrum-e-werk/)

One of Germany's larger socio-cultural centres, at Fuchsenwiese 1: four event rooms, a cinema and a beer garden. It hosts concerts, student parties and club nights; see its [party page](https://www.e-werk.de/programm/partys/).

## Good to know

-   Programmes and entry prices change every week. Check the venue's own page on the day you go.
-   The E-Werk cinema shows films in the original language with subtitles (OmU).

Local sources checked 22 October 2026. Check the linked pages for current prices and rules.
