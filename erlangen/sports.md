# Erlangen - Sports

## For FAU students: Hochschulsport

[FAU Hochschulsport](https://www.hochschulsport.fau.de/startseite/) runs more than 150 courses each semester, from team sports to fitness, outdoor and relaxation.

Registration is online only and opens about 7 days before courses start. Use your @fau.de email address and have your IBAN ready; the bank must take part in SEPA.

Every course has a fee. Students pay the lowest rate, plus a €2 administration fee per participation. See the [participation rules](https://www.hochschulsport.fau.de/teilnahmeinformationen/).

### Browse the [course list](https://www.hochschulsport.fau.de/kurse-classes/)

Booking is limited: usually at most three offers per category and five in total per person. The site is being redesigned; if a page looks broken, use the Courses menu.

Local sources checked 22 October 2026. Check the linked pages for current prices and rules.
