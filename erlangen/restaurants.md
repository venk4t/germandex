# Erlangen - Restaurants

## On campus

### [Südmensa](https://www.werkswelt.de/?id=sued)

Studierendenwerk dining hall at Erwin-Rommel-Straße 60, with a cafeteria and a café bar. The daily menu and opening hours are on its page; hours change in the semester break.

### Other Studierendenwerk outlets

The [Studierendenwerk](https://werkswelt.de/index.php?id=mensen-cafeterien-cafebars) also lists Mensa Langemarckplatz and Cafeteria Kochstraße in Erlangen. Check each page for the current menu and hours.

### Vegetarian or vegan?

Mensa menus mark vegetarian and vegan dishes. Check the menu for the day.
