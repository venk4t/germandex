# Aachen - Party

### Pontstraße

The student bar street. Start your night here. Poststraße has a lot of bars too.

### [Apollo](https://apollo-aachen.de/club/events/)

The big mainstream club, Pontstraße 141-149, at the Ponttor. Mainstream music, theme nights, and the big welcome parties every October.

### [Musikbunker](https://mubu.ac/)

Concerts and club nights in an old bunker. The locals' pick if you want something less mainstream.

### Barbarossa

Cheap drinks. Karaoke on Wednesdays.

### LikeLike

Tiki bar near Pontstraße with cheap and unusual wheat beers.

### Sowiso

Good music, plus cards, darts and billiards.

## Good to know

-   October is party season: watch the [AStA](https://www.asta.rwth-aachen.de/) calendar for the Semesteranfangsparty and the "Students United" welcome party at Apollo. Last year's had €5 entry before midnight and free beer until 1 am.
-   Locals say skip the "Nightlife" club near Rothe Erde.
-   For proper big-club nights, people go to Cologne, about an hour away by train.
