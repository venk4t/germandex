# Aachen - Restaurants

## Recommended

### [AKL](https://www.akl-orient.de/)

Lebanese food in a relaxed, family-style place. Pontstraße 1-3. Mon-Sat 11:30-23:00, closed Sundays.

### [TAT Gemüsekebab](https://www.tat-gemuesekebab.de/restaurants) €9.60

Best Döner and Dürüm in Aachen. At Markt 44 and at Elisenbrunnen (Friedrich-Wilhelm-Platz 11). Open from 11:00, late on weekends.

### [Al Triangolo](http://www.al-triangolo.de/)

Small family-run Italian in the old town, Rommelsgasse 2-3. Dinner place. Closed Tuesdays.

### Vegetarian? These are your best bets

For veggie kebab, try TAT or [Ağa Gemüsekebab](https://xn--aga-gemsekebab-msb.de/) at Franzstraße 3. Ağa is a good pick for grilled vegetables or halloumi.

## Cheap and quick

### [Nobis](https://nobis-printen.de/oeffnungszeiten/)

Aachen's own bakery chain, with branches all over town. Sandwiches, coffee and Printen (the local gingerbread). The café on Münsterplatz sits right by the cathedral.

### Subway

You know it. Reliable, cheap, filling.

### Bäckerei [Moss](https://baeckerei-moss.de/filiale/)

Aachen bakery with branches across town. Bread rolls, sandwiches, cakes and coffee; MOSS auf der Hörn on Ahornstr. 42 is near RWTH.
