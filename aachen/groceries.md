# Aachen - Supermarkets

For the chains and how to choose, read our [supermarket guide for Germany](https://germandex.com/germany/groceries). Local international stores are below.

## Asian & international supermarkets

For spices, rice, lentils and everything else from back home: Aachen has a few Asian and international stores, mostly around Jülicher Straße and Hansemannplatz.

### New Sherona Supermarket

Heinrichsallee 30, near Kaiserplatz. Indian, Pakistani and African groceries: spices, rice, lentils, frozen food and fresh produce. Open daily.

### Nagammaal Markt

Jülicher Str. 4, near Hansemannplatz. Long-running Indian and Asian grocery store. Open Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 10 pm.

### [Asian aiX Bazaar](https://www.aix-bazaar.de/)

Jülicher Str. 20. South Asian and African groceries, fresh produce, halal fish and a fish cutting service.
