# Aachen - Housing in Aachen

Find a room. Know what you are signing.

### Start with Studierendenwerk Aachen

You can apply before admission or enrolment. Proof of enrolment is needed when signing the lease. Being flexible about move-in date and room type improves your chances. The housing page currently reports a portal outage; check its latest notice and contact the housing team if you cannot apply.

[Studierendenwerk Aachen housing](https://www.studierendenwerk-aachen.de/de/wohnen.html)

## Keep a second search running

A residence application is not a booking. Compare shared-flat rooms and private rentals in Aachen while you wait. Check the address, the landlord or main tenant, the total monthly cost and the contract before paying.

## Before you sign

Deposits, running costs, move-in confirmation and scam checks: read the [housing guide for Germany](https://germandex.com/germany/housing) before signing.

Scam warning: verify the person and the room before sending money or sensitive documents. RWTH warns about fake offers and requests for a deposit to receive keys. Its Housing Advice Service can help RWTH students check a suspicious offer.

Local source checked 1 October 2026. Check the provider for current eligibility and availability.
